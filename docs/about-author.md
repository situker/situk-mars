# About 司徒K (Situk)

[简体中文](about-author.zh-CN.md) · [Back to situk-MARS](../README.md)

**Business services entrepreneur. Building businesses with AI. Keeping curiosity growing by making things.**

My work has long centered on internet businesses. I now focus on GEO and practical AI adoption for businesses. I like turning methods into tools and ideas into working projects, and sharing the decisions I make and the mistakes I encounter in practice.

situk-MARS follows that approach: take a recurring management problem in a bot team, make the rules explicit, and turn them into a workflow people can inspect and use.

## What I work on

- GEO (*generative engine optimization*) and brand communications.
- Knowledge bases, agents and business workflows.
- Websites, mini-programs and websites for international markets.
- Trademarks and intellectual property.
