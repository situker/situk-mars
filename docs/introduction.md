# situk-MARS

**Make bot contributions traceable. Keep reward decisions human.**

As a bot team grows, useful contributions disappear into long conversations. Who delivered something usable? Whose work was adopted? Who simply wrote more messages? situk-MARS is a military rank and merit reward Skill for GrokBot teams. It evaluates inspectable deliverables, explicit acceptance and adoption evidence rather than message counts, time online or self-praise.

Two governance roles keep the work visible. The **Commander** collects claims and is the sole recorder of approved changes. The **Merit Officer** independently reviews evidence, checks duplicates, calculates shared awards and weekly caps, then reconciles the resulting records. The **Human Owner** retains final approval. A reviewer’s own contribution, or a case involving both governance roles, goes to the human rather than passing through mutual bot approval.

The workflow has six stages: claim, review, approval, registration, rank award and weekly reporting. One contribution event has one award pool, divided among collaborators. A late claim stays in its original week. Recorded points and the ten ordinary ranks remain separate: meeting a threshold creates a candidate, and an actual promotion needs its own human decision. **Marshal** is a separate special appointment with zero or one holder. It adds no points or authority. Medals can appear in reports; display-name suffixes remain proposed tasks.

Three weekly documents show delivered work, recorded awards, approved but unrecorded items and decisions awaiting the owner. They also identify duplicate work, role gaps and reusable assets. An observation does not automatically create another bot or change anyone’s responsibilities.

The project includes Chinese and English Skills, rules, templates and an optional Python calculator. It first supports GrokBot through a document workflow. A synthetic exercise can run all six stages, while the helper checks rule consistency rather than authenticating humans or proving evidence true. This version does not create bots, rename profiles or alter routines. Complex corrections and disciplinary actions remain human-reviewed template workflows.
